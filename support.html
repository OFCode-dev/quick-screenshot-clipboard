Troubleshooting

  • Nothing happens when I click the icon. Some pages cannot be captured for security reasons (browser settings pages, the extension store, local PDF viewers). Try a regular website, or reload the tab and try again.
  • The screenshot is not on my clipboard. Clipboard access can be blocked by the page or your operating system. Click somewhere on the page first so the tab has focus, then try again.
  • The image looks blurry or cropped. The extension captures the visible area of the tab at your current zoom level. Reset the page zoom to 100% for best results.

Reporting a bug

Please include your browser and version, the extension version, and what you expected versus what happened. This makes fixes much faster.
